Righteous Branch of Christ Ministry

Rooted in Jeremiah 23:5 and John 15:5, Righteous Branch of Christ Ministry is devoted to verse-by-verse biblical teaching, prophetic Scripture study, worship in spirit and truth, and equipping believers as the Temple of the Living God.

Through expository sermons, parallel multilingual Scripture study, daily devotional reflections, and sacred congregational hymns, our fellowship seeks to exalt the Lord Jesus Christ—the Righteous Branch of David—and strengthen the body of Christ across nations and languages.
